## 2. What these numbers do and don't show

- **261 matches the plan's maximum request count:** 3 rounds × 75 requests, plus the optional 36
  v3 USD requests in round 1 = 261 (plan §4). That is consistency with the plan, not a check of the
  results.
- **HTTP 200 is not evidence of valid fares.** A 200 can carry `success: false`, an empty `data`,
  `no_data`, one-way or wrong-month offers, or non-TWD amounts. Those are the plan's actual
  criteria (currency, round trip, month, airports, error mapping, token safety), and the tool
  records them in `requests.jsonl` and `summary.json`, which this repository doesn't have.
- **HTTP 200 is not evidence of commercial suitability.** Suitability depends on coverage and
  price agreement per route and month (plan §5 B). The owner reports CONDITIONAL, which by the
  plan's definition means v3 covers at least half of the covered route/months but misses some
  that v1 covers.
- **The A = PASS verdict is the owner's reading** of the tool's summary. It can't be reproduced
  from the repository.

## 3. Missing evidence

Not in the repository, and not invented here:

1. Per-round `summary.json` / `summary.md` (`b5_compare.py summarize`).
2. Coverage per API, route and month; the route/months where v3 missed fares v1 found.
3. Price agreement between v1 and v3; fare stability across the three rounds.
4. Currency evidence (response `currency` fields; the round-1 TWD/USD ratio).
5. Rejection counts by reason (one-way, wrong month, past departure, airport mismatch).
6. `market=tw` comparison results.
7. Result of each round's secret scan (`check-output`): token, `@`, account identifiers.
8. Round dates and the commit the tool ran from.

**To upgrade this record to verified:** the owner runs, locally,
`py -3.12 aws\tools\b5_compare.py check-output b5-results\roundN` for each round and then
`summarize b5-results\round1 b5-results\round2 b5-results\round3 --out b5-results\b5-summary.json`
([tool guide](m1.4-b5-tool-guide.md)), scans the summary for the
token, `@` and account identifiers, and commits **only** the summary (never `requests.jsonl`).
No new live request is needed for that.
